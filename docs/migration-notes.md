# Migration notes — Unicorn Club

## What came across

All 19 of Patrick's candidates, with their statuses, match lines, bios, private
notes, referral sources, Instagram handles, city groupings and manual ordering.
Generated by `scripts/build-seed.py` from `candidates.json`; IDs are UUIDv5, so
re-running produces identical output.

The five city tabs came across by **label**, not by key: the original data had
drifted (key `la` was labelled "Miami", `aspen` was labelled "Los Angeles"), and
the label is what the matchmaker actually reads. Keys were re-derived from
labels.

## What did not

**Photos.** 18 of the 19 candidates have a photo, embedded as base64 data URIs
inside `Pat's Roster (standalone, with photos).html`. That file was not among
the ones handed over, so the photos are not in the seed. To bring them in:
parse that file for `<image-slot id="photo-{candidateId}">`, decode each data
URI, upload to a `candidate-photos` bucket, and set `candidate.photo_url`. The
candidate IDs in that file are the *original* ids (`s1`, `u1785186971998`, …);
map them through the same UUIDv5 derivation `scripts/build-seed.py` uses.

**localStorage.** The live data was in the browser, not in the source files. If
the roster in her browser has moved on since `candidates.json` was exported, the
export needs redoing before this seed is treated as current.

## One thing worth her attention

Splitting the model surfaced something the old tool's shape hid. A few
client-facing `about` paragraphs read like internal assessments:

- **Natalia** — "passing for now because though she filled out the questionnaire
  she hasn't responded to me yet"
- **Micaela E** — "Just turned 40 so likely too old but she is lovely"
- **Johanna D** — "A few years older than ideal so will hold for now"

In the old tool `about`, `blurb`, `status` and `note` sat side by side on one
record, so it was easy for a working judgment to land in the field the client
reads. Under the new model those sentences belong in the private note. They have
been carried over **exactly as written** rather than edited — that is her copy
and her call — but they are worth a pass before the roster is shared again.
